# Example: Continuation Loop

## Turn 1: vague idea

> I want to build an AI and video system for building-material factories.

Route: **Positioning**.

The Skill should first turn the broad capability request into a few paid workflows, such as factory product video production, sales enablement, or dealer lead conversion. It should recommend one narrow entry point and ask one question that changes the choice.

## Turn 2: user feedback

> The factory has many products and dealers often ask for product details.

Route: **Commercial validation** or **MVP execution**, depending on whether a real pilot customer exists.

The Skill should not restart the full repository search. It should use the new information to define the first content unit, buyer, input materials, and success metric.

## Turn 3: clear pilot

> A sales manager is willing to try it for one product line.

Route: **MVP execution**.

The output should be a small pilot: product files and raw clips in, reviewed short videos and sales-ready product cards out, with a measurable lead or response-time metric.
